Section 1 • Problem Context

1. Problem Statement (SIH26167)

Challenges in Modern Earth Observation, Satellite Image Exploitation & Geospatial Analysis

⚡
1. Satellite Data Deluge & Human Bottleneck
Petabytes of bi-temporal satellite imagery are captured daily across Sentinel, Landsat, and commercial satellites. Manual photo-interpretation requires certified GIS analysts and takes hours to days per scene, creating fatal bottlenecks in disaster response and defense.
Data Inundation
☁️
2. Sensor Modality Fragmentation
Optical sensors are blinded by heavy clouds, haze, and nighttime. Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) penetrates weather but suffers from heavy multiplicative speckle noise, making cross-modal synthesis notoriously difficult.
Optical + SAR Mismatch
🗣️
3. Lack of Natural Language Interactivity
Traditional GIS software (QGIS, ArcGIS) has a steep learning curve. Field commanders, urban planners, and disaster responders cannot ask intuitive questions like "What buildings were constructed since January?" or "Count storage tanks in sector 4".
No Conversational GIS
🎯
4. Hallucinations & Uncertainty in Generic AI
Generic Vision-Language Models (VLMs) hallucinate geographic facts and lack pixel-coordinate grounding, geospatial coordinate reference systems (CRS), and verifiable scientific confidence scoring needed for defense and governance.
Zero-Trust Scientific Need
Section 2 • System Introduction

2. Introduction to SatQuery AI

A Unified Multimodal Intelligence Platform for Next-Gen Remote Sensing

  • ✓
    Siamese Vision Transformer (ChangeFormerV6): Hierarchical encoder-decoder operating on paired bi-temporal captures ($T_1, T_2$) to achieve 98.65% F1-score with jet difference probability heatmaps.
  • ✓
    Remote Sensing Visual QA (RS-VQA): Supports multi-task natural language queries across 23 land-cover categories, object counting, and semantic verification.
  • ✓
    Open-Vocabulary Spatial Text Grounding: Dynamic feature localization emitting pixel bounding boxes $[y_{min}, x_{min}, y_{max}, x_{max}]$ and mask overlays for queried targets.
  • ✓
    Optical + SAR Cross-Modal Fusion: Despeckles SAR via Lee filtering and synthesizes Excess Green (ExG) and NDWI indices for all-weather, cloud-penetrating vision.
  • ✓
    Native Geospatial GeoTIFF & CRS Pipeline: Powered by rasterio and shapely to translate raw pixel contours directly into georeferenced WGS84 / UTM polygons.
  • ✓
    JARVIS 2.0 Autonomous Analyst Copilot: Local agent orchestrator powered by Ollama reasoning and Neural Text-to-Speech (TTS) for hands-free operational briefings.
  • ✓
    Scientific Confidence Engine: Replaces black-box heuristics with mathematically calibrated uncertainty derived from softmax margins, spatial entropy, and cross-modal agreement.
  • ✓
    GIS Interoperability: Instant 1-click export of detected changes into standard GeoJSON FeatureCollections and formatted analytical reports.

3. Services We Have Used

Modular Microservice Architecture Spanning Deep Learning, GIS, and Agent Orchestration
